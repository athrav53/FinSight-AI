EXPENSE CATEGORIZATION AND SPENDING ANALYSIS GUIDE

WHAT IS EXPENSE CATEGORIZATION?

Expense categorization is the process of grouping
financial transactions into meaningful categories.

It helps individuals understand spending patterns,
monitor budgets, and identify major expenses.

COMMON EXPENSE CATEGORIES

1. Housing:
   Rent, mortgage payments, and property maintenance.

2. Groceries:
   Food and household essentials purchased from stores.

3. Transportation:
   Fuel, public transport, parking, and vehicle expenses.

4. Utilities:
   Electricity, water, gas, and internet bills.

5. Healthcare:
   Medical appointments, medicines, and healthcare expenses.

6. Entertainment:
   Movies, streaming services, and recreational activities.

7. Shopping:
   Clothing, electronics, and other retail purchases.

8. Education:
   Course fees, books, and educational subscriptions.

9. Insurance:
   Health, vehicle, home, and other insurance premiums.

10. Other:
    Transactions that cannot be confidently classified.

NEEDS VS WANTS

Needs are essential expenses required for daily living.

Examples:
- Basic housing
- Essential groceries
- Necessary transportation
- Utilities

Wants are discretionary expenses that may be reduced
or postponed.

Examples:
- Optional entertainment
- Luxury purchases
- Nonessential subscriptions

The classification of needs and wants depends
on individual circumstances.

EXPENSE CATEGORY PERCENTAGE

Category Spending Percentage =
(Category Expenses / Total Expenses) * 100

EXAMPLE

Total Monthly Expenses: Rs. 40,000
Groceries Expenses: Rs. 8,000

Groceries Percentage =
(8,000 / 40,000) * 100

Groceries Percentage = 20%

TOP SPENDING CATEGORIES

Top spending categories are the expense groups
with the highest total recorded spending.

They help identify where most money is being spent.

MONTHLY EXPENSE COMPARISON

Monthly expense comparison examines spending
changes between different months.

EXAMPLE

January Groceries: Rs. 6,000
February Groceries: Rs. 7,500

Expense Change = 7,500 - 6,000

Expense Change = Rs. 1,500

Percentage Change =
((7,500 - 6,000) / 6,000) * 100

Percentage Change = 25%

AI-BASED EXPENSE CATEGORIZATION

AI and rule-based systems can classify transactions
using merchant names and transaction descriptions.

EXAMPLES

Transaction: UBER TRIP
Suggested Category: Transportation

Transaction: NETFLIX
Suggested Category: Entertainment

Transaction: SUPERMARKET PURCHASE
Suggested Category: Groceries

Transaction: ELECTRICITY BILL
Suggested Category: Utilities

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS

Merchant names may be ambiguous.

One merchant may sell products belonging
to multiple categories.

Transaction descriptions may be incomplete.

Automated categorization can produce errors.

Users should be able to review and correct
automatically assigned categories.

Transfers between personal accounts should not
automatically be categorized as expenses.

Refunds should be handled separately to avoid
overstating total spending.

IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS

A high expense in one category does not
automatically indicate poor financial management.

Spending patterns should be interpreted
using available transaction records.

Missing transactions may affect analysis accuracy.

This document provides general financial
education and not personalized financial advice.
