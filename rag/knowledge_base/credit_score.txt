CREDIT SCORE AND CREDIT HISTORY GUIDE

WHAT IS A CREDIT SCORE?

A credit score is a numerical indicator used to help
assess a person's creditworthiness.

Credit scores are calculated using information from
credit reports and scoring models.

Different countries, credit bureaus, and scoring models
may use different score ranges and calculations.

FACTORS THAT MAY AFFECT CREDIT SCORES

1. Payment History:
   Records of whether credit payments were made on time.

2. Credit Utilization:
   The proportion of available revolving credit currently used.

3. Credit History Length:
   The duration of an individual's recorded credit history.

4. Credit Mix:
   The different types of credit accounts held.

5. Recent Credit Applications:
   Certain credit applications may result in hard inquiries.

CREDIT UTILIZATION FORMULA

Credit Utilization (%) =
(Total Revolving Credit Balance / Total Revolving Credit Limit) * 100

EXAMPLE

A person has a credit card limit of Rs. 100,000
and an outstanding balance of Rs. 25,000.

Credit Utilization = (25,000 / 100,000) * 100

Credit Utilization = 25%

WHY CREDIT HISTORY MATTERS

Lenders may use credit information when evaluating
applications for loans, mortgages, and credit cards.

A credit score alone does not guarantee approval
or determine the exact interest rate offered.

WAYS TO MAINTAIN HEALTHY CREDIT HABITS

1. Make required payments on time.
2. Review credit reports for inaccuracies.
3. Monitor outstanding balances.
4. Avoid unnecessary credit applications.
5. Understand borrowing terms before accepting credit.

IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS

A high credit score does not guarantee loan approval.

Checking your own credit report generally does not
affect your credit score.

Credit scoring practices vary across countries
and financial institutions.
