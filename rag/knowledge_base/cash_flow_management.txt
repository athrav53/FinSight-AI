CASH FLOW MANAGEMENT GUIDE

WHAT IS CASH FLOW?

Cash flow refers to the movement of money
into and out of an individual's finances
during a specific period.

Cash inflows represent money received.

Cash outflows represent money spent.

TYPES OF CASH INFLOWS

1. Salary and wages
2. Freelancing income
3. Business income
4. Rental income
5. Interest income
6. Other recorded income

TYPES OF CASH OUTFLOWS

1. Housing expenses
2. Groceries
3. Transportation
4. Utility bills
5. Loan repayments
6. Insurance payments
7. Entertainment expenses

NET CASH FLOW

Net Cash Flow = Total Cash Inflows - Total Cash Outflows

POSITIVE CASH FLOW

Positive cash flow occurs when total inflows
are greater than total outflows.

EXAMPLE

Monthly Income: Rs. 60,000
Monthly Expenses: Rs. 45,000

Net Cash Flow = 60,000 - 45,000

Net Cash Flow = Rs. 15,000

NEGATIVE CASH FLOW

Negative cash flow occurs when total outflows
exceed total inflows.

EXAMPLE

Monthly Income: Rs. 40,000
Monthly Expenses: Rs. 48,000

Net Cash Flow = 40,000 - 48,000

Net Cash Flow = Rs. -8,000

CASH FLOW FORECASTING

Cash flow forecasting estimates future
cash inflows and outflows.

Forecasting helps identify periods when
available cash may be insufficient.

A basic cash flow forecast considers:

1. Expected income
2. Recurring expenses
3. Upcoming bills
4. Loan repayments
5. Planned purchases
6. Expected savings contributions

CASH FLOW MANAGEMENT BEST PRACTICES

1. Record income and expenses regularly.
2. Separate recurring and irregular expenses.
3. Monitor upcoming payment dates.
4. Review monthly cash flow trends.
5. Maintain accessible emergency savings.
6. Update forecasts when circumstances change.

IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS

Positive cash flow does not necessarily mean
that an individual has no outstanding debt.

Negative cash flow does not automatically
indicate financial distress.

Bank transaction records may be incomplete.

Transfers between personal accounts should
not automatically be treated as income.

Cash flow forecasts are estimates and
actual results may differ.
