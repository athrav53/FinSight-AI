SAVINGS GOALS AND FINANCIAL PLANNING GUIDE

WHAT IS A SAVINGS GOAL?

A savings goal is a specific amount of money that
a person plans to save for a future purpose.

Savings goals help individuals organize their
financial priorities and monitor progress.

TYPES OF SAVINGS GOALS

1. Short-Term Goals:
   Goals expected to be achieved within one year.
   Examples include purchasing electronics,
   travel expenses, and festival spending.

2. Medium-Term Goals:
   Goals expected to be achieved within one to five years.
   Examples include vehicle purchases,
   professional education, and home deposits.

3. Long-Term Goals:
   Goals expected to take more than five years.
   Examples include retirement savings
   and long-term financial independence.

SMART SAVINGS GOALS

A SMART savings goal should be:

Specific
Measurable
Achievable
Relevant
Time-bound

SAVINGS CALCULATIONS

Monthly Savings Required =
(Target Savings Amount - Current Savings) /
Number of Months Remaining

EXAMPLE

Target Savings: Rs. 120,000
Current Savings: Rs. 20,000
Time Remaining: 10 months

Monthly Savings Required =
(120,000 - 20,000) / 10

Monthly Savings Required = Rs. 10,000

This calculation assumes no interest,
investment returns, fees, or withdrawals.

SAVINGS RATE

Savings Rate (%) =
(Monthly Savings / Monthly Income) * 100

EXAMPLE

Monthly Income: Rs. 50,000
Monthly Savings: Rs. 10,000

Savings Rate = (10,000 / 50,000) * 100

Savings Rate = 20%

BEST PRACTICES FOR SAVINGS

1. Define a clear savings target.
2. Set a realistic deadline.
3. Track savings progress regularly.
4. Automate transfers when practical.
5. Review savings goals when income changes.
6. Keep emergency savings separate from planned expenses.

IMPORTANT CONSIDERATIONS

Savings goals should account for essential
living expenses and financial obligations.

Long-term goals may be affected by inflation.

Investment returns are uncertain and should
not be assumed to be guaranteed.
